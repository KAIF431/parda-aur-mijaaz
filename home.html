The Core Story

Beneath The Veil Of Silence

01

“Parda Aur Mijaz” is a profound exploration of unspoken devotion, quiet dignity, boundaries, and the values that shape a person from within. It is a story where silence speaks louder than words, where modesty is not merely something worn, but something deeply lived.

At its heart are Haya and Mijaz — two concepts that reveal the difference between what the world sees and what the soul carries within. Haya is more than modesty; it is the quiet awareness of one's worth, boundaries, and presence. Mijaz is more than temperament; it is the character reflected in one's choices, silence, patience, and principles.

ہر ملاقات ایک سوال ہے، ہر خاموشی ایک جواب، اور ہر پردہ اپنے پیچھے ایک الگ دنیا رکھتا ہے۔

Every meeting leaves behind a question. Every silence carries an answer. And every veil hides a world that not everyone is meant to see.

Because sometimes, the deepest stories are not told through words. They exist in lowered gazes, unspoken feelings, restrained emotions, and the courage to remain true to one's values when no one is watching.

Parda Aur Mijaz is not simply about what is hidden from the world. It is about discovering what lies beneath the silence — the emotions, principles, devotion, and strength that quietly define a person.

The Mind Behind The Novel

About The Author

Mohammad

Writer & Storyteller

Mohammad is an imaginative and emotionally driven writer who finds stories in ordinary moments. His writing focuses on love, faith, family, friendship, modesty, and the emotions that quietly shape a person's life.

He believes that the most beautiful stories are not always about extraordinary people, but about ordinary people experiencing extraordinary emotions. His imagination often explores the connection between love and spirituality, where feelings are not merely about attraction, but about respect, patience, and the search for something pure.

Through his stories, Mohammad tries to turn simple moments into memories that stay with the reader long after the story ends.

Protagonists & Dynamics

Characters & Souls

01

Fahad

Fahad is a simple, mature and family-oriented young man. He is calm and responsible, but becomes playful and carefree around his close friends, especially Qasim. He is connected to his faith and values prayer, respect, modesty and family. He is not someone who easily gets attracted by looks; he believes in controlling his emotions and keeping things within halal boundaries. Deep down, he is sensitive and thoughtful, but he takes time to understand his own feelings.

02

Aamina

Aamina is a modest, reserved and dignified girl. She observes hijab and niqab and keeps a respectful distance from unnecessary attention. Her personality reflects haya, adab and devotion to her faith. She is quiet but carries a strong sense of self-respect. Fahad has never seen her face, yet her modesty and the way she carries herself slowly leave an impression on his heart. Her character is defined more by her values than her appearance.
