Chapter 01

Ek Khwaab

Calculating... Calculating...

"Har mohabbat mulaqaat se shuru nahi hoti... Kuch mohabbatein Allah ek khwaab ke zariye dil mein utaar deta hai."

Us raat shehar mein kuch bhi alag nahi tha.

Mumbai apni raftaar se chal raha tha.

Ye shehar kabhi poori tarah sota nahi tha.

Raat ke is waqt bhi kahin na kahin roshni jal rahi hoti...

Kisi sadak par gaadiyan daud rahi hoti...

Kisi station par log aakhri local pakadne ke liye bhaag rahe hote...

Aur kisi chhoti si chai ki dukaan par din bhar ki thakan liye kuch log ab bhi baithe hote.

Mumbai mein har chehra apne saath ek kahani lekar chalta tha.

Koi apne gaon se hazaaron umeedein lekar aaya tha...

Koi apne maa-baap ka sahara banne ke liye...

Aur koi sirf isliye ke kal aaj se behtar ho.

Is shehar mein khwab dekhna aasaan tha...

Lekin un khwabon ko poora karne ke liye har roz khud se ladna padta tha.

Subah hone se pehle ghar se nikalna...

Lambi bheed ka hissa banna...

Local ki bheed mein khade hokar safar karna...

Din bhar kaam karna...

Aur phir shaam ko wahi thaka hua badan lekar ghar lautna.

Kisi ke paas waqt nahi tha.

Kisi ke paas paisa kam tha.

Kisi ke paas dono nahi the.

Lekin phir bhi...

Har subah log uthte the.

Naye din ki umeed lekar.

Mumbai ki sabse ajeeb baat shayad yehi thi...

Ke yahan thak jaana aam tha...

Magar ruk jaana nahi.

Har shakhs bhaag raha tha.

Koi apni manzil ki taraf...

Koi apne ghar ki taraf...

Aur koi sirf guzarti hui zindagi ko sambhalne ki koshish mein.

Isi bheed mein...

Isi shehar mein...

Aur isi sab ke beech...

Ek Fahad bhi tha.

Raat apni jagah thi...

Hawa apni jagah.

Kahin dukaanon ke shutter gir rahe the.

Kahin sadkon par din bhar ki bheed dheere dheere kam ho rahi thi.

Kuch log apne gharon ki taraf laut rahe the, aur kuch abhi bhi apne roz ke kaamon mein masroof the.

Shehar ke kisi kone mein kisi ghar se bachche ko sulaane ke liye lori ki halki si awaaz aa rahi thi.

Fahad bhi apne ghar ke chhote se kamre mein din bhar ki thakan liye bistar par aa gaya tha.

Aaj bhi din baaki dinon jaisa hi guzra tha.

Subah daftar jaana...

Roz ka kaam karna...

Aur shaam ko ghar wapas aa jaana.

Fahad ki zindagi mein filhaal isi tarah ke din the.

Woh zyada khwahishen rakhne wala insaan nahi tha.

Jo mila...

Us par Allah ka shukr karta.

Aur jo nahi mila...

Uske liye bechain rehne ke bajaye apne Rab par bharosa karta.

Mizaj mein sanjeedgi thi...

Lekin apne logon ke saath bilkul alag tha.

Ghar mein Ammi se baat karte waqt uski awaaz mein narmi aa jaati...

Abbu ke saamne adab aur lihaz rehta...

Aur behen ke saath kabhi kabhi bachpana bhi laut aata.

Doston mein bhi woh zyada bolne walon mein se nahi tha.

Lekin jis se apnapan ho jaata...

Uske saath ghanton baith sakta tha.

Deen uski zindagi ka sirf ek hissa nahi tha.

Woh apni zindagi ke faislon ko bhi usi nazariye se dekhta tha.

Halal aur haram ka farq uske liye sirf lafzon ka farq nahi tha.

Uski koshish hoti ke jo bhi kare...

Sahi tareeqe se kare.

Uski duniya bahut badi nahi thi.

Ghar...

Naukri...

Kuch apne log...

Aur chand aise dost jin par woh bharosa karta tha.

Isi mein uska waqt guzarta tha.

Na kisi se shikwa...

Na zindagi se koi badi shikayat.

Na koi khaas waqia hua tha...

Na kisi baat ne uske zehan ko der tak apne andar uljha kar rakha tha.

Bas ek aur aam din.

Fahad ki zindagi aisi hi thi.

Saadah...

Khamosh...

Aur apni chhoti chhoti masroofiyat mein poori.

Usne kabhi zindagi se bahut kuch nahi maanga tha.

Uske liye halal rizq mil jaana hi ek badi nemat thi.

Ghar mein sukoon ho...

Apne log khush rahen...

Aur Allah usse apni raza wale raaste par rakhe.

Uski khwahishein bhi isi ke aas paas ghoomti thin.

Duniya ki chamak-dhamak ne kabhi use zyada mutaasir nahi kiya tha.

Woh apni zindagi se mutmain tha.

Aur shayad...

Isi liye usse apni ye saadgi achhi lagti thi.

Aur us raat bhi...

Usse lag raha tha ke kal subah uthkar phir wahi sab hoga.

Wahi daftar...

Wahi kaam...

Wahi log...

Aur Mumbai ki wahi bhaagti hui zindagi.

Us raat bhi Isha ki namaz ke baad Fahad apne kamre mein aa gaya.

Ghar mein dheere dheere khamoshi utarne lagi thi.

Ammi kitchen ka aakhri kaam samet rahi thi.

Abbu bhi apne kamre mein ja chuke the.

Fahad kuch der apne bistar ke paas baitha raha.

Phir usne Qur'an uthaya aur tilawat shuru kar di.

Kamre mein uski awaaz bahut halki thi...

Magar raat ki khamoshi mein har lafz saaf sunai de raha tha.

Kuch der baad usne Qur'an band kiya.

Use apni jagah par rakha...

Aur dono haath utha kar apne Rab ka shukr ada kiya.

Uske paas maangne ke liye koi lambi fehrist nahi thi.

Bas wahi chhoti chhoti duayein...

Apne ghar walon ke liye...

Apne rizq ke liye...

Aur apni zindagi mein sukoon ke liye.

Dua ke baad woh kuch der khamosh baitha raha.

Phir uth kar kamre ki batti bujha di.

Kamra andhere mein doob gaya.

Sirf khidki se bahar ki halki roshni andar aa rahi thi.

Parde hawa ke saath dheere dheere hil rahe the.

Deewar par lagi ghadi ki tik-tik...

Khamoshi mein aur zyada numayan lag rahi thi.

Fahad bistar par let gaya.

Kuch der chhat ki taraf dekhta raha.

Phir ek karwat li.

Aaj ke din ke baare mein socha...

Kal ke kaam ke baare mein bhi.

Lekin aakhir mein zehan bhi thak gaya.

Usne aankhen band kar li.

Bahar kahin door se kisi gaadi ke guzarte hue awaaz aayi...

Phir woh bhi khamoshi mein gum ho gayi.

Fahad ne gehri saans li.

Aur dheere dheere uski aankhon par neend utarne lagi.

Kuch hi der mein...

Woh gehri neend mein ja chuka tha.

Aur phir...

Uski neend ne ek aisi duniya ka darwaza khola...

Jahan sab kuch haqeeqat jaisa tha.

Magar haqeeqat nahi tha.

Usne khud ko ek roshan jagah par khada dekha.

Har taraf ajeeb sa sukoon tha.

Na koi shor tha...

Na koi awaaz.

Bas ek gehri khamoshi thi...

Aur us khamoshi mein uska dil jaise apne Rab ke aur qareeb hota ja raha tha.

Usne apne aas paas dekha.

Sab kuch jaana pehchana sa lag raha tha...

Lekin phir bhi...

Jaise woh kisi aisi jagah par tha jahan woh pehle kabhi nahi aaya tha.

Uske dil par ek ajeeb si kaifiyat taari thi...

Jise alfaaz mein bayan karna mushkil tha.

Na use kisi cheez ki fikr thi...

Na kisi cheez ki talab.

Bas dil mein ek sukoon tha.

Phir woh sajde mein chala gaya.

Lamba sajda...

Aisa sajda jisme insaan apne Rab ke sabse qareeb hota hai.

Uska dil bhar aaya.

Jaise andar chhupi hui saari baatein ek saath bahar nikalna chahti hon.

Aankhon se aansu behne lage.

Woh kuch der tak sajde mein hi raha.

Phir dheere se sar uthaya.

Namaz mukammal hui.

Usne dono haath dua ke liye utha diye.

Kuch pal tak woh khamosh raha.

Jaise use khud samajh na aa raha ho...

Ke maange to kya maange.

Phir...

Us waqt uske dil mein sirf ek hi naam tha.

Aamina.

Naam zubaan par aate hi uski aankhon mein phir se aansu aa gaye.

Woh khud bhi samajh nahi pa raha tha...

Ke jis ladki ke baare mein usne kabhi is nazariye se socha tak nahi...

Aaj usi ka naam uski dua ka hissa kaise ban gaya.

Uski zubaan laraz rahi thi.

"Aamina..."

Phir usne aankhen band kar li.

"Ya Allah..."

"Agar yeh mere liye behtar hai..."

"To Aamina ko mera naseeb bana de."

"Main nahi jaanta ke mere liye kya behtar hai..."

"Tu jaanta hai."

"Main nahi jaanta ke aage kya hoga..."

"Tu jaanta hai."

"Bas agar is rishte mein meri duniya aur meri aakhirat ki bhalai hai..."

"To is raaste ko mere liye aasaan farma."

Uski awaaz dheere dheere bharra gayi.

"Hum dono ke dilon mein ek dusre ke liye mohabbat paida farma."

"Humare ghar walon ke dilon mein raza daal de."

"Agar is rishte mein khair hai..."

"To humare liye halal tareeqe se isko mukammal farma."

"Aur agar yeh mere liye behtar nahi..."

"To mere dil ko bhi apne faisle par raazi kar de."

Har dua ke saath uski hichkiyan aur tez hoti ja rahi thin.

Uske aansu rukne ka naam hi nahi le rahe the.

Woh baar baar apne Rab se wahi baat keh raha tha...

Jaise uske paas maangne ke liye aur kuch tha hi nahi.

Usne pehle kabhi kisi ke liye is tarah dua nahi maangi thi.

Na kabhi kisi ko apne naseeb mein maanga tha...

Na kabhi kisi ke liye apne dil ko itna bechain mehsoos kiya tha.

Aur shayad isi liye...

Woh khud bhi is khwaab ko samajh nahi pa raha tha.

Uski dua abhi jaari thi...

Aankhon se aansu abhi bhi beh rahe the...

Aur uske honton par Aamina ka naam tha.

Phir...

Achanak...

Sab kuch dhundhla hone laga.

Roshni dheere dheere door jaane lagi.

Uski awaaz jaise kisi gehri khamoshi mein doobne lagi.

Aur agle hi pal...

Uski aankh khul gayi.

Saans tez chal rahi thi.

Kamra waise hi khamosh tha.

Deewar par lagi ghadi ab bhi chal rahi thi.

Khidki se thandi hawa ab bhi andar aa rahi thi.

Sab kuch waise hi tha...

Jaise uske aankhen band karne se pehle tha.

Lekin...

Fahad waise nahi raha tha.

Usne beikhtiyaar apne gaalon ko chhua.

Ungliyan geeli ho gayin.

Woh kuch lamhon tak apni ungliyon par lage un aansuon ko dekhta raha.

Jaise yaqeen hi nahi aa raha ho...

Ke woh sach mein ro raha tha.

Phir dheere se bistar par baith gaya.

Uska dil ab bhi tez dhadak raha tha.

Zehan mein wahi manzar baar baar aa raha tha...

Wahi jagah...

Wahi dua...

Aur Aamina.

Uska dil ek hi sawaal baar baar pooch raha tha...

"Aakhir yeh hua kya hai?"

Usne kabhi Aamina ke baare mein is tarah socha hi nahi tha.

Na kabhi uske saath apna mustaqbil tasawwur kiya tha.

Na kabhi uske liye dil mein koi aisa jazba mehsoos hua tha...

Phir ek hi raat mein...

Sab kuch kaise badal gaya?

Woh kuch der tak khamosh baitha raha.

Na usse dobara neend aa rahi thi...

Na hi woh us khwaab ko apne zehan se nikaal pa raha tha.

Usse bilkul andaza nahi tha...

Ke yeh sirf ek khwaab ka asar nahi tha.

Yeh us safar ki ibtida thi...

Jo uske dil ko mohabbat...

Sabr...

Aur khamoshi ke sabse mushkil imtihaan se guzarne wala tha.
