Chapter 12

Khamosh Fasla

Calculating... Calculating...

Waqt kabhi ek jaisa nahi rehta.

Uski chaal bohot aahista hoti hai.

Itni aahista...

Ke insaan ko uski aahat bhi mehsoos nahi hoti.

Pata hi nahi chala...

Ke kab unki lambi guftagu...

Chhote chhote jumlon mein badalne lagi.

"Ye file bhej dijiye."

"Ji."

"Mail ho gaya?"

"Haan."

"Report complete?"

"Nahi."

Aur phir...

Bas...

"Haan."

"Nahi."

"Ji."

"Theek hai."

Jaise alfaaz unse rooth gaye hon.

Jaise har guftagu apne mukhtasar anjaam tak simat gayi ho.

Office waise hi chal raha tha.

Subah attendance...

Phir mails...

Clients ki queries...

Reports...

Team mein pehle ki tarah hansi mazaak bhi hota.

Kabhi kisi ko Excel ka formula samajh nahi aata.

Kabhi Sales Team kisi destination ko lekar ulajh jaati.

Kabhi koi Fahad ko awaaz deta.

"Bhai... ek minute."

Aur woh roz ki tarah sabke kaam mein masroof rehta.

Sab kuch pehle jaisa hi tha.

Bas...

Ek cheez pehle jaisi nahi rahi thi.

Aamina aur Fahad ki guftagu.

Ab dono ke darmiyan sirf utni hi baat hoti...

Jitni kaam ke liye zaroori hoti.

Na usse kam...

Na usse zyada.

Agar kisi teesre shakhs ne un dono ko dekha hota...

To shayad use kabhi mehsoos hi na hota...

Ke unke darmiyan kuch badla bhi hai.

Kyuki unki guftagu pehle bhi zyada nahi hoti thi.

Aur ab bhi...

Kaam rukta nahi tha.

Bas...

Lehje badal gaye the.

Kabhi kabhi...

Fahad beikhtiyaar uski taraf dekh leta.

Magar foran apni nazrein wapas screen par le aata.

Use maloom tha...

Har khamoshi ka ek sabab hota hai.

Aur har sabab...

Har kisi ko bataya nahi jaata.

Isliye usne kabhi koi sawal nahi kiya.

Na kabhi us tabdeeli ka zikr chheda.

Na kabhi us khamoshi ko todne ki koshish ki.

Aamina bhi pehle ki tarah apne kaam mein masroof rehti.

Jo kehna hota...

Sirf kaam ke mutaalliq kehti.

Aur phir dobara apni duniya mein laut jaati.

Din guzarte rahe.

Office waise hi chalta raha.

Magar un dono ke darmiyan...

Jaise koi deewar khadi ho chuki thi.

Ek aisi deewar...

Jise dono dekh to sakte the...

Magar gira nahi sakte the.

Na Fahad ne kabhi is tabdeeli ka sabab poochha.

Na Aamina ne kabhi koi wajah batayi.

Dono ne bas...

Is khamoshi ko qabool kar liya.

Aur kabhi kabhi...

Khamoshi hi woh baat keh deti hai...

Jo alfaaz saari zindagi nahi keh paate.

ek din...

Subah ki meeting khatam hote hi Fahad ne ek report tayyar ki.

Usne report Aamina ko bheji .

"Ye final report hai."

Aamina ne sirf ek nazar daali.

"Theek hai."

Bas itna hi.

Na pehle ki tarah...

"Thanks."

Na...

"Main check kar leti hoon."

Sirf...

"Theek hai."

Kabhi kabhi...

Office mein aise lamhe bhi aate...

Jab poori team ek saath hans padti.

Koi purana qissa chhed deta.

Koi kisi ki taang kheench deta.

Aur kuch der ke liye poora floor hansi se bhar jaata.

Fahad bhi muskurata tha.

Aamina bhi kabhi kabhi halki si muskurati thi.

Magar...

Ab un dono ki muskurahaton ka ek doosre se koi ta'alluq nahi raha tha.

Din guzarte gaye.

Kaam bhi hota raha.

Meetings bhi.

Reports bhi.

Sab kuch waise hi tha...

Jaise kuch badla hi na ho.

Magar Fahad jaanta tha...

Sab badal chuka hai.

Aur Aamina bhi...

Shayad ye baat usse zyada achhi tarah jaanti thi.

Kabhi kabhi...

Fahad beikhtiyaar kisi baat ke liye uski taraf dekh leta.

Lekin agle hi lamhe...

Khud hi apni nazrein hata leta.

Ab usne umeed karna bhi chhod diya tha...

Ke shayad sab pehle jaisa ho jayega.

Kuch khamoshiyan...

Wapas alfaaz tak nahi lautti.

Aur kuch fasle...

Sirf qadam badhane se khatam nahi hote.

Isi tarah...

Mahine guzarte gaye.

Bas...

"Haan."

"Naa."

"Ji."

"Theek hai."

Yehi alfaaz un dono ke darmiyan reh gaye.

Magar...

Kahani yahin khatam nahi hoti.

Ajeeb baat to uske baad shuru hui.

Fahad ne kai martaba mehsoos kiya...

Ke Aamina uski taraf dekh rahi hai.

Jab woh kisi file mein masroof hota...

Ya computer ki screen par nazrein jamaaye baitha hota...

Use beikhtiyaar ehsaas hota...

Jaise koi usse dekh raha ho.

Aur jab woh apni nazar uthata...

To Aamina foran apni nazrein pher leti.

Pehli baar usne ise ittefaq samjha.

Doosri baar bhi.

Magar jab ye silsila baar baar dohraya gaya...

To usne samajh liya...

Ke ye sirf ittefaq nahi tha.

Kabhi kabhi...

Aamina ki nazrein bhi Fahad ki nazron se takra jaati.

Magar sirf chand lamhon ke liye.

Aur phir...

Woh foran apni palken jhuka leti.

Na Fahad mein itni himmat thi...

Ke der tak uski aankhon mein dekh sake.

Na Aamina mein.

Bas...

Ek pal ke liye nazrein milti.

Aur agle hi pal...

Dono apni apni duniya mein laut jaate.

Lekin...

Un chand lamhon mein...

Na jaane kitni ankahi baatein guzar jaati thin.

Arsa guzar chuka tha.

Lekin na woh pehle jaisi guftagu baaqi rahi...

Na woh pehle jaisi be-takallufi.

Aakhir...

Aisa kya hua tha?

Kis cheez ne un dono ke darmiyan itna faasla paida kar diya?

Aaj bhi...

Jab Fahad un dinon ko yaad karta hai...

To uske zehan mein yehi sawal ubharta hai.

Na koi jhagda hua.

Na koi shikayat.

Na kabhi koi unchi awaaz.

Phir bhi...

Do log...

Jo roz ek doosre se khul kar baat karte the...

Ek din sirf...

"Haan."

"Naa."

Aur...

"Ji."

Tak kaise simat gaye?

Shayad...

Har kahani ka dushman koi insaan nahi hota.

Kabhi...

Waqt.

Kabhi...

Halaat.

Aur kabhi...

Ek aisi khamoshi...

Jo dheere dheere do dhadakte dilon ke darmiyan utar jaati hai.

Aur jab khamoshi bolna shuru kar de...

To alfaaz...

Apni ahmiyat kho dete hain.

hayad...

Har kahani ka ek mod hota hai.

Hamari kahani ka bhi tha.

Farq sirf itna tha...

Ke us mod par na kisi ne awaaz di...

Na kisi ne rukne ko kaha.

Bas...

Do log...

Ek hi daftar mein rehte hue bhi...

Ek doosre se bohot door chale gaye.

Aur uss doori ki wajah...

Abhi baaqi thi.
